LambdaLab

A simply-typed lambda calculus whose parser and printer are proved inverse in Lean 4. Enter a program and it is parsed, elaborated, and printed back — metavariables such as ?0 solved, parentheses normalised. Anything it cannot read is just error; real diagnostics do not exist yet. 

Press Run.
or press ⌘/Ctrl + Enter
